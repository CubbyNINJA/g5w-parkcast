# Ambiguity, contradiction and blocker register

Nothing here is decided. A "proposal" describes a suggestion only: it isn't adopted until the
owner accepts it, and then it moves to `DECISIONS.md`. Scenarios that assert a proposal carry
its `@AMB-nn` tag.

Types: **A** consequential ambiguity · **B** blocker · **M** missing acceptance criterion.

| ID | Type | Question | Evidence | Proposal | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AMB-01 | A | Must video and audio be impossible outside Park? | OD-01 says "when parked". The stock unit locks out video while driving (`com.hkmc.regulation`; DiPO listens for `android.mobis.regulation.ACTION_EXIT_REGULATION`). | Play only in Park. Any error, missing service or unknown firmware stops both video and audio. Shifting out of Park stops playback at once. | DECIDED: OD-23 (video parked-only; audio allowed outside Park) |
