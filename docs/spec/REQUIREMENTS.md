# Requirements

Requirement rows (`AIR-nnn`) and their acceptance tests (`AT-nn`). Rows are drafted by Astra
(`docs/spec/reviews/ASTRA_REQUIREMENTS_*.md`) from the owner decisions in `DECISIONS.md`, and
copied here with any wording change noted. Open questions stay in `AMBIGUITY_REGISTER.md`.

| AIR-031 | (a) The S1 tools MUST be one command, `python tools/s1.py <command>`, with the commands `check`, `build`, `run`, `analyse`, `evaluate` and `summary`. | AIR-033 | (a) Every `run` invocation that launched UxPlay MUST end by reminding the owner how to remove the firewall allowance, whatever its ending (`owner ended`, `timeout`, `uxplay exited`, `capture failed`, `interrupted`, or a launch that failed after the process was created). (e) If the run record can't be written, the reminder MUST still be given, and the command MUST NOT claim a record was made. (f) Giving the reminder MUST NOT change the command's exit code. | OD-31, OD-33, OD-35, OD-37, OD-38. |
