## Rationale

AIR-071: OD-178 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:186`) accepts the texts drafted in `specs/features/14_receiver_tools.feature:15-27`. They are copied in verbatim, with pipes escaped. (e) carries over its numbering rule. (f) stops a logger prefix from being read as part of the text.

AIR-075: (b) now names the two markers that decide whether a log is complete, so the evaluator matches on fixed text (OD-178).

AIR-076: (a) and (b) now refer to the exact codec and first-frame markers. (e) keeps `owner-note.txt` to one line, as OD-179 (`DECISIONS.md:187`) requires. Because the checklist sits next to the note, a stray `picture:` line in it must not become a GO (OD-154, `DECISIONS.md:162`), so it is fail-safe.

AIR-079: (e) encodes OD-179's file, its place, its line format and its stick template. The six keys cover exactly the five "owner's note" observations in AT-50/51/54/80/81 plus OD-173's CarPlay check (`DECISIONS.md:181`; `specs/features/15_receiver_unit.feature:33,39,45,170-188`). The parsing rules in (f) mirror the note rules in AIR-076(e) and are fail-safe under OD-86 (`DECISIONS.md:94`): an unclear line never passes a check. (g) is the old (e), now pointing at the named file. `sound on unit` stays non-failing under AIR-054(d) (`REQUIREMENTS.md:64`).

AT rows: every `@unit` "owner's note" criterion that is not about the picture now reads `run-checklist.txt`. AT-79 gains the checklist parsing fixtures and the template check. AT-76 gains the check that the checklist cannot be mistaken for the note.

## Reversibility and safety

None of these changes installs anything new or changes anything on the PC or the unit. The template is a text file on the stick, and the evaluator reads the checklist on the PC; deleting the file undoes it. The receiver's behaviour is unchanged (AIR-079(g)). The CarPlay-after-exit gate stays fail-safe: a missing result blocks further on-unit runs.
