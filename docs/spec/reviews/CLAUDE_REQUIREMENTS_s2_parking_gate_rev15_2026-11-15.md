## Reversibility and safety

- **What changes:** specification text only. Nothing was built, installed or run, and no repository file was edited. Working copies of the rows were generated only in this session's temporary scratchpad, outside the repository.
- **Runtime effect of AIR-041:** it uses only the receiver's own focus callback and adds `covered=` log records. It needs no permission and stores nothing between app starts.
- **Ship-gate evidence:** comes from the read-only `camera-probe` and the receiver's own log, which write only to the stick.
- **Undo:** remove AIR-041 and AT-41, and revert clause (j) and the cover exceptions in AIR-019 and AIR-040.
- **Safety direction:** covering only suppresses or removes notices. It never permits video, moves the gate, or touches audio or the iPhone connection.
- **Remaining exposure:**
  - A notice can be missed or held, which is owner-visible.
  - A window whose removal keeps failing can stay over the camera (open question 4).
  - Two stacked windows cover more of the screen than one.
  - A notice already on screen can sit over the camera for up to about 1 s before focus moves (`CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:43-44`, open question 3).
  - Stock windows that never take focus are not detected (open question 5).

## Open questions

AMB numbers are not assigned. The next free one is AMB-82.

1. **Hidden and held notices when focus returns (owner-visible).** The draft does not show again a notice that covering removed. A held attempt is made when the receiver is uncovered, for the full 5 s. Other options: show the removed notice again, show it only for its remaining time, or drop held attempts. Dropping held attempts breaks OD-101's report. Under any option, a receiver whose window never gains focus never shows a notice.
2. **Where a lone logging-failure notice sits (owner-visible).** The draft keeps it in the raised slot even when it is alone. Other options: move it down when the other notice goes, which needs `updateViewLayout`, or place the registration-failure notice below instead.
3. **`REVERSE_ON` as an extra, earlier cover trigger (safety).** It would close the gap of about 1 s before the camera takes focus. It is not sticky, so the camera state at process start would be unknown (`CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:38-39`, `:43-44`). The draft uses focus alone.
4. **A window that cannot be removed while the camera is up (safety).** OD-119 allows two removal calls, and OD-117 says remove at once. The draft makes another call at each cover start. Other options: OD-119's count strictly, keep retrying while covered, or end the receiver.
5. **Cases focus does not cover (safety).** Surround view (SVM) and the digital rear-view mirror (DRVM) were not exercised. Stock windows above the camera that take no focus (`Heads Up`, `SS_OSD_Window`) are not detected (`CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:29-32`, `:45-46`).
6. **Scope of the ship gate.** What counts as shipping, for example staging the receiver on the owner's stick for the AT-41 run itself, and whether any release is gated beyond AT-41's `@unit` pass.
