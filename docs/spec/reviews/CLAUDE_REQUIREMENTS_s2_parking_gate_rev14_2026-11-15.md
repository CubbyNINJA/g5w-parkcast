## Rationale

**AIR-040(e), form.** OD-115 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:123`) says both notices are "a toast-style window (same look as a toast, a toast-type window needing no extra permission on 4.4) removed after exactly 5 s", and replaces the "short Android toast" form. That makes revision 13's `Toast.LENGTH_SHORT` text toast and its overlay ban (`REQUIREMENTS.md:50`) obsolete; the overlay ban would forbid the decided form. "Toast-type window" is encoded as `TYPE_TOAST`. "Same look as a toast" is encoded as the platform's own toast view for the text, which gives the unit's own toast styling, and the toast flags that stop the window taking focus or touches. Leaving out `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON`, which stock toasts carry, is fail-safe under OD-86 (`DECISIONS.md:94`): a notice shouldn't change display power state, including while asleep. Banning `Toast.show()` stops a second, shorter copy of the same notice. "No extra permission" is encoded as no `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` declared for notices, which a manifest scan can check.

**AIR-040(e), text and timing.** OD-112 (`DECISIONS.md:120`, AMB-75 at `AMBIGUITY_REGISTER.md:85`) gives the logging-failure text, copied byte for byte. The old "MUST differ" rule is dropped because the two fixed texts differ, and OD-104's "assert only that they differ" (`DECISIONS.md:112`) no longer applies. OD-114 (`DECISIONS.md:122`, AMB-77 at `AMBIGUITY_REGISTER.md:87`) makes revision 13's first-attempt timing owner policy. The text in both rows is unchanged; only the citation changes. The 5,000 ms is measured from the add call's return on the receipt-age clock, the clock revision 13 already uses for the retry, with an exact `S+4999`/`S+5000` boundary. Removal is unaffected by ACC changes, logging and the other notice. All of these are fail-safe timing choices under OD-86, made the same way as OD-111 (`DECISIONS.md:119`).

**AIR-040(f), fails to show and the 5 s retry.** OD-111's rule (only a throwing create or show call fails) carries over to the window: the create-view and add-window calls. The retry timer starts at a failed attempt, which leaves nothing on screen, and the display timer starts at a successful add. So for one notice the two never overlap: a notice failing at `F` and shown on retry is on screen from `F+5000` to `F+10000`. OD-113 (`DECISIONS.md:121`, AMB-76 at `AMBIGUITY_REGISTER.md:86`) makes revision 13's "fire regardless of sleep" owner policy and is now stated outright. The registration-failure retry always falls due while asleep (AIR-019(h)), so AIR-019(i) says so too.

**AIR-040(g), both on screen.** Both can appear at the same clock time: a registration failure whose `ACC registration failed` write also fails (AIR-040(c)). Independent windows and timers follow from OD-58 and OD-101's separate allowances (`DECISIONS.md:66`, `:109`) and OD-111's "neither moves the other". Placement is what the owner sees. Two toast views at the default toast position would sit exactly on top of each other, so the later one would hide the earlier for up to 5 s. The draft therefore requires different positions only, as a proposal (open question 1).

**AIR-040(h), failed removal.** On 4.4, nothing but the receiver removes a `TYPE_TOAST` window an app added itself. The system timeout covers only toasts sent through `Toast.show()` (not verified on the unit). So a failed removal could leave a window up for the rest of the run. Mirroring OD-107, the draft makes one more removal call 5 s later and then waits for exit (open question 2). Counting the notice as shown and not treating the failure as a logging failure follow OD-111's matching rules for show failures. That keeps once-per-start (OD-58) and stops notices cascading.

**AIR-040(i), exit.** OD-111 and revision 13 already say exit doesn't wait for a notice retry (`REQUIREMENTS.md:50`). Removing any window at once and completing exit even if that removal throws keeps that rule and leaves no window behind the receiver. It's a fail-safe choice under OD-86. The exact-tie rule (one call when exit and removal coincide) matches revision 13's `F+5000` exit case.

**AIR-019(i).** Only the form wording, the sleep note, pointers to AIR-040(g) to (i), and the side-effect list change. The OD-106 text (`DECISIONS.md:114`) and the OD-107 retry (`DECISIONS.md:115`) stand, as OD-115 says.

**AT-19 and AT-40.** Every new clause has an `@offline` oracle on the UI double's add and remove log, with exact boundaries (`S+4999`/`S+5000`, `F+10000`, `S+9999`/`S+10000`). The `@unit` criteria only ask for observations by eye or by time, because `specs/README.md:39-42` forbids captures of the phone's contents.

## Reversibility and safety

- These are specification text changes only. Nothing is built, installed or run on the PC or the head unit, and this subagent edited no file.
- At runtime a notice adds one transient `TYPE_TOAST` window. It doesn't take focus or touches, declares no permission, writes nothing and persists nothing. Exit removes it, and so does the end of the process in stock Android (not verified on the unit). Reverting to a standard toast changes only AIR-040(e) to (i), the AIR-019(i) pointer and the two AT rows.
- The safety direction is unchanged. A notice never permits video, never blocks it by itself, never moves the gate, and never starts polling or retries registration.
- New exposure: a notice now stays up 5 s instead of about 2 s, and a failed removal can leave it up longer. Because the window takes no touches, it can't block head-unit controls, but it can cover part of the screen (open question 3).

## Open questions

1. **Two notices on screen together.** When both notices are up at once, for example a registration failure whose `ACC registration failed` write fails, where do they go? At the default toast position the later one hides the earlier for up to 5 s. The draft requires only different positions (AIR-040(g)). Options: stack the logging-failure notice above the registration-failure notice; show the second only after the first is removed (which delays one notice, against OD-114's "when detected"); or accept the overlap. Changes what the owner sees.
2. **A window that won't go away.** If the removal at 5 s throws, the draft tries once more 5 s later and then leaves the window until exit (AIR-040(h)). Options: that; keep retrying every 5 s until it goes or exit; or no second try. Changes what the owner sees.
3. **Notice over the reversing camera or other safety display.** A `TYPE_TOAST` window can draw above ordinary app windows. Whether it can cover the rear-camera image or a system warning on this unit isn't known. OD-115 lengthens that exposure from about 2 s to 5 s. Should a notice be suppressed, or removed early, while such a display is up? This touches safety. The draft doesn't address it, so a decision is needed before the slice ships.

## Not verified

- **`TYPE_TOAST` without permission on this unit.** Stock 4.4 `PhoneWindowManager.checkAddPermission` lets any app add `TYPE_TOAST`. That is recalled from AOSP, not read here. The unit's 260206 framework wasn't checked: OEM binaries weren't opened, per the role file.
- **Removal is the app's job on 4.4.** It's assumed the 4.4 system doesn't time out a `TYPE_TOAST` window added directly, and that it removes a dead process's windows. Both are from AOSP memory, not checked on the unit.
- **Stock toast flags.** That KitKat's `Toast.TN` uses `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON | FLAG_NOT_FOCUSABLE | FLAG_NOT_TOUCHABLE` is recalled, not read.
- **How removal can fail.** A `removeView` from a thread other than the one that added the window, or on a window the system already removed, throws on stock Android. Whether either can happen in the receiver depends on design that isn't written yet.
- **Look on the unit.** Whether the platform toast view on the OEM build looks like the unit's own toasts, and where it sits on the screen (relevant to open question 1).
- **Display and clock across sleep.** Carried from revision 13 (`CLAUDE_REQUIREMENTS_s2_parking_gate_rev13_2026-11-15.md:70-71`): whether anything is visible during ACC `6`, `7` or `255`, and whether the receipt-age clock keeps advancing in deep sleep. That decides when a removal that falls due during sleep actually happens.
- **Reverse-camera layering** (open question 3): not known from any evidence file read.
- **Other files.** `specs/features/*.feature` and `docs/spec/TRACEABILITY.md` were not read, so these rows aren't checked against the scenarios. No critic agent was run, because the role is read-only with a single output.
- **New OD and AMB numbers.** The fail-safe choices here (window flags and view, the 5,000 ms clock and boundary, AIR-040(g) to (i)) still need recording as an OD-86 decision. The three open questions need AMB numbers. Neither is assigned in this draft.
