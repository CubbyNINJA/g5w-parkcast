## Rationale

**AIR-019.** Requirement text unchanged; it already holds a registration-failure attempt while covered "when AIR-041(d) makes it" (`docs/spec/REQUIREMENTS.md:29`). OD-122 settles revision 15 open question 1 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:130`, `docs/spec/reviews/CLAUDE_REQUIREMENTS_s2_parking_gate_rev15_2026-11-15.md:65-75`), so the dependency sentence becomes a citation. OD-121, OD-124 and OD-125 are added because covering now includes `REVERSE_ON` and the 1 s retry, both through AIR-041 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:129,132,133`). AT-19 is unchanged and not repeated.

**AIR-040.** Clause (g) gains OD-123's rule: a lone logging-failure notice keeps its raised `y` until its own removal (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:131`). Clause (h)'s exception now points at AIR-041(b)'s OD-125 calls (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:133`). OD-125 does not change the uncovered OD-119 rule (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:127`). The "revision 15 Rationale" pointer becomes OD-121 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:129`). AT-40 gains one OD-123 assertion and is otherwise unchanged.

**AIR-041.** Clause (a) adds OD-124 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:132`). Covering runs from each `REVERSE_ON` receipt to the first focus gain delivered after it. That answers the prompt's cases:
- **Already unfocused:** the receiver stays covered until a gain after the receipt.
- **Focused, with no later loss:** it stays covered until a gain or exit. That is OD-124's text ("until the receiver's window regains focus"), held under OD-86.
- **`REVERSE_OFF`:** plays no part (clause (g)), because OD-124 does not use it and no `REVERSE_OFF` sender exists in automotive-service (`docs/evidence/CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:11`).

On the evidence run, `REVERSE_ON` came about 1 s before `AvmCamera` took focus, and neither broadcast is sticky (`docs/evidence/CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:16-27,34-39`; `docs/spec/AMBIGUITY_REGISTER.md:94`). The rest of the row:
- **Clause (j):** registers at runtime and unregisters at exit, like the ACC broadcast. Its registration-failure handling is a proposal (open question 1).
- **Clause (b):** encodes OD-125. Timing the post-uncover try from the uncover time is a timing edge resolved fail-safe under OD-86 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:94`).
- **Clause (f):** its tokens are logging edges, also under OD-86.
- **Clauses (c), (d):** already match OD-122.
- **Clause (h):** encodes OD-127 (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:135`).
- **Clause (i):** encodes OD-126; the non-focus overlays are listed at `docs/evidence/CAMERA_SIGNAL_FINDINGS.md:29-32` (`docs/spec/DECISIONS.md:134`).

AT-41 adds a broadcast double, the OD-125 timing cases and the `REVERSE_ON` cases. Its `@unit` part becomes a public-release gate, with OD-126's unexercised list.

## Reversibility and safety

These are specification rows only, and nothing is installed or changed by drafting them. At runtime, AIR-041(j) adds one runtime broadcast receiver, which is unregistered at exit. It needs no permission and no manifest entry, and persists nothing on the unit. Notice windows are still removed at exit under AIR-040(i). After a registration failure, the receiver is covered for the rest of that app start, which suppresses notices only. None of the changes touch the gate, video, audio or the iPhone connection (AIR-041(e)). A notice that cannot be removed can stay on screen until exit, as OD-119 and OD-125 accept. The publication gate (AIR-041(h)) still blocks a public release until the `@unit` run passes.

## Open questions

1. **Failed `REVERSE_ON` registration (AIR-041(j)).** The drafted proposal: if `registerReceiver` throws, the receiver counts as covered for the whole app start. No notice is shown in that run, including the registration-failure and logging-failure notices, and one record is logged. The alternative is to fall back to focus-only covering, which is revision 15's behaviour and leaves the ~1 s gap. This touches safety and what the owner sees.
2. **`REVERSE_ON` never reaching a third-party runtime receiver on the unit.** It might be protected or need a permission. AT-41 `@unit` then fails, and the receiver cannot be released under OD-127. Does the owner want a fallback (for example focus-only covering), or does the block stand? This touches safety and scope.
