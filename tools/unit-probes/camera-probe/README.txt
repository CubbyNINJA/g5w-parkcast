camera-probe - READ-ONLY, about two minutes. How can the receiver tell the reversing camera is on
screen (OD-117)? Records camera-related broadcasts and log lines, and the top window each second,
while you shift to R and back. Guarded to build 260206.
RUN: car in Park and switched on (ready), in a safe place, foot on the brake. Copy this folder's
NEMESIS.data to the drive root, insert, tap Yes. When the toast says "shift to R", shift to R for about
10 s, then back to P. Wait for "Camera probe done". Removes the root NEMESIS.data when it starts.
Output: NEMESIS-diag/camera-probe/. Never commit it.
